Three Pillars of the Programme (SOP -6)

Pillar I — Periodic Health Check-upBaseline assessment at joining + Periodic health assessment every 180 days (90 days for comorbid staff and staff aged ≥45).
Pillar II — Occupational Health SurveillanceOccupational risk assessment, vaccination, exposure monitoring & preventive action.
Pillar III — Staff Health & Well-beingWeekly compulsory wellness hour, health education, physical activity & mental-health promotion.
